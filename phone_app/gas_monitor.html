How to connect

Android: pair HC-05 in Bluetooth settings first (PIN 1234 or 0000). Close any other Bluetooth terminal app, because the HC-05 accepts only one connection. Allow Chrome the Nearby devices permission. Open this page from its https link in Chrome 137 or newer (not from WhatsApp or a file manager), then tap Connect HC-05.

Laptop: pair HC-05 in Windows Bluetooth settings, open this file in Chrome or Edge, tap Connect HC-05 and pick the HC-05 COM port.

iPhone and Safari cannot connect to the HC-05. Demo works everywhere.
